## Mercer CCBHC video

Mercer's behavioral health video series includes a CCBHC Model discussion with Dr. Jason Vogler, Jon Villasurda, and Toby Ramsdell. The discussion focuses on how states can implement and sustain Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinics by aligning policy, operations, and payment.
