# Jon Villasurda Jr. at the 2026 CCBHC Annual State Meeting

Mercer Government featured **Jon G. Villasurda Jr., MPH**, as an invited breakout-session facilitator at the **2026 CCBHC Annual State Meeting**.

The public announcement highlighted Jon's experience in Medicaid and behavioral health and described a peer discussion focused on helping states:

- Integrate the Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinic model into behavioral health systems
- Navigate common policy and implementation challenges
- Advance long-term behavioral health system goals
