# About Jon Villasurda Jr., MPH

<img class="profile-image" src="{{ site.author.image | escape }}" alt="Portrait of Jon Villasurda Jr." width="180" height="180" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Jon Villasurda Jr. is a Senior Principal with Mercer Government based in Okemos, Michigan. He works across Medicaid, behavioral health, and public-sector health strategy, with an emphasis on turning policy into workable operating, financing, measurement, and evaluation approaches.

Public sources may list him professionally as **Jon Villasurda** or **Jon Villasurda Jr.**

## Current work

- Senior Principal with Mercer Government
- Medicaid and public-sector health strategy
- Behavioral health services and delivery system design
- Analytics, finance, performance measurement, and program evaluation

## Previous experience

Jon's previous public-sector and association experience includes the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services and Michigan Primary Care Association. His work has included Medicaid policy, program operations, strategic initiatives, and healthcare delivery design.

## Education

Jon earned a Bachelor of Science in psychology and a Master of Public Health in health management and policy from the University of Michigan.
