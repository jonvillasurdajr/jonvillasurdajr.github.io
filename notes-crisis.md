# Behavioral Health Crisis Systems Notes

Behavioral health crisis systems require more than a list of services. Program design has to connect access, triage, stabilization, follow-up, financing, and accountability.

## Design considerations

- Define how people enter the crisis continuum and how handoffs occur.
- Identify the roles of call centers, mobile response, receiving facilities, providers, and payers.
- Track follow-up and continuity after the immediate crisis episode.
- Align financing and data collection with the operating model.
