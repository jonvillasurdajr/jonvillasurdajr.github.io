# Healthcare Performance Measure Notes

*By [Jon Villasurda Jr., MPH](/about/)*

Measures are more useful when their purpose is explicit.

## Measure roles

- Operating measures show whether workflows are functioning now.
- Quality measures show whether care meets intended standards.
- Finance measures show resource use and cost patterns.
- Evaluation measures test whether a program changed outcomes or tradeoffs.

## Practical checks

- Define numerator, denominator, timing, and data source.
- Identify who will act on the result.
- Avoid treating every available metric as a key performance measure.
- Revisit measures when policy design or workflows change.
